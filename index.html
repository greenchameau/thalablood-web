About ThalaBlood

ThalaBlood is a free, offline personal health assistant designed specifically for Thalassemia patients — built by a Thalassemia patient.

Managing transfusion schedules, blood test results, and daily medications can be overwhelming. ThalaBlood makes it simple, organised, and stress-free — all on your phone, with no account required.

Available on Android via Google Play.

Features

Transfusion Tracking Log every transfusion with Hb and ferritin values. Highlights your next expected date.
Medication Reminders Track daily medications with dose and frequency reminders.
Lab Results Store Hb and ferritin measurements over time with trend charts.
Medical Calendar Log appointments, scans, and events. View upcoming at a glance.
PDF Reports Export a full yearly health report as a PDF to share with your doctor.
Fully Offline No internet needed. All data stays on your device.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

ThalaBlood does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or health data to external servers or third parties. All information you enter — transfusion records, medication schedules, lab results, and medical events — is stored exclusively on your device.

The app works entirely offline and does not require an internet connection, an account, or any personal information to use.

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any third-party SDKs that collect user data. The optional backup feature exports your data as a file to your own device — it is never sent anywhere automatically.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. For questions, contact us at thalabloodapp@gmail.com.
